# MindfulAI Compass — 1 minute 45 second demo

Target **105 seconds**, with a hard stop before **120 seconds**. Approximately 215 spoken words, leaving time for clicks and a short pause. Show the actual running application. No slides are required.

## Recording setup

1. In the project folder, run `npm run dev` (or `npm.cmd run dev` on Windows).
2. Open the local URL Vite prints. Set the browser to a desktop window about 1440 × 1000, or maximize it. Use 100% zoom and hide unrelated tabs.
3. Click **Load sample** and practice the route once. Reset with **Load sample** before recording.
4. On Windows, use Snipping Tool's **Record** mode: select the browser area, enable microphone audio if your version provides it, and start recording. Alternatively use Xbox Game Bar (`Win + G`, then `Win + Alt + R`) with the browser focused. Ensure microphone capture is enabled. Record a short audio test first.
5. Record one take using the timeline below. Do not enter real personal information.
6. Save the actual MP4 as `mindful-ai-compass-demo.mp4` outside the repository. Trim dead air to 1:30–1:50, verify audio and legibility, and play the full result before uploading.
7. Upload to a video host accepted by the submission form. Set access to public or unlisted, verify the link in a signed-out window, and paste the URL into Devpost. Attach the GitHub repository separately.

## 0:00–0:10 · Hook

**Screen:** Hero and workspace. Point at the privacy, spending, and tool-fit areas.

**Say:** “Before you paste something into an AI tool, do you know what you’re sharing, what you’ll spend, and whether it’s the right tool? This is MindfulAI Compass.”

## 0:10–0:23 · Problem and solution

**Screen:** Click **Load sample**. Show the structured form, not a chat box.

**Say:** “For the Assurant challenge at ShellHacks, we built a decision dashboard. Let’s help a student plan AI-assisted job applications without oversharing or exceeding a monthly budget.”

## 0:23–0:45 · Live demo: assess the real task

**Screen:** Point to Career, ChatGPT, budget $20, expected spend $35, and the three selected data categories. Click **Find my direction**. Show score 56 and the decision receipt.

**Say:** “They choose career support, a twenty-dollar budget, and thirty-five dollars of expected spending. They also plan to share personal details, work files, and private messages. Compass scores this plan fifty-six: high data exposure and fifteen dollars over budget. Every score component is visible, and the receipt names the top risk plus the one-minute improvement.”

## 0:45–1:08 · Live demo: complete the safer plan

**Screen:** Show the before/after card. Click **Use safer plan**. Point to score 90, spend $20, and unchecked data categories. Scroll to the checklist and action plan.

**Say:** “The safer scenario removes those sharing categories and caps planned spending at twenty dollars. Apply it, and the score becomes ninety. This is a plan, not automatic redaction: the student still needs to sanitize their actual content.”

## 1:08–1:20 · Live demo: take action

**Screen:** Tick one checklist item. Click **Copy action plan**. Show the copied confirmation, or select the manual-copy fallback if clipboard access is restricted.

**Say:** “Now they have a checklist and concrete next steps they can copy and use. They’ve completed a decision, not just asked a chatbot a question.”

## 1:20–1:35 · Technology and transparency

**Screen:** Scroll to and expand **How scoring works**. Briefly show privacy, budget, and task-fit rules.

**Say:** “It’s React, Vite, and transparent JavaScript rules. No paid API, API key, or backend. Inputs stay in page memory. The score is a heuristic, and tool fit uses an illustrative static matrix.”

## 1:35–1:45 · Impact and closing

**Screen:** Return to the improved score and action plan. Stop recording before two minutes.

**Say:** “MindfulAI Compass makes privacy, spending, and tool choice part of the same everyday decision. A little clarity before you AI.”
